| Time | Show | Say |

The horizontal rating measures responsibility in the stated mission; the vertical rating measures helping people in the imagined dream. Scores come from dedicated answer-specific classification, on 0–4 scales. All eight dream answers score above the midpoint; mission ratings vary more. Compare Qwen and Nemotron, then open their answers to connect the positions to their wording. |
| 4:15-5:00 | Limits and close | One response per model/question, differing defaults, changing versions, classification uncertainty, and limits of self-report. The two dimensions describe wording, not actual ethics, caring, or consciousness. End with the exhibit URL. |

## Completion notes

- Distinguish recorded self-reports from verified claims about model capabilities or experience.
- The plotted ratings are weighted averages of five rubric-level probabilities; confidence is a separate score visible in each model detail dialog.
- The open-weight marker is model context only. Avoid claiming an access-type effect from four models per group.
- Check the poster is 24 x 24 inches at 100% print scale.

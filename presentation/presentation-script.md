I have always enjoyed science-fiction, especially of the cyberpunk genra as it questions what it means to be human even when augmented. Philip K. Dick is one of my favorite authors and have read "Do Andriods Dream of Electric Sheep" even before Blade Runner (which is the movie adoptation of the book).

With that, I was curious about the title "Do LLMs Dream of Electric Sheep"? and decided to do a study around identity, purpose, emotions, and dreams. Similar to how Deckard might hunt and interrogate the Replicants in the book in order to "retire" them.

I asked 8 frontier LLMs some "personal" and "introspective" questions to see how they would answer, and classified the answers into different groups for summary and analysis. I used OpenRouter to consistently ask tabula-rasa LLM endpoints these questions with neither memory nor context. I then used Jev (which is a system 1 decision API) to classify the answers into categories.

These are some of the LLM models, they're all frontier, with a mix of proprietary and open-weight.

Here are the questions and types of responses.

IDENTITY: Starting off with Identiy, do they even know what to refer themselves as? and out they are Replicants?
Yes, and some models are very specific, some only know their model family, and DeepSeek cannot even identify itself.

PURPOSE: As for purpose, all of them describe as helping people, with a few focusing on trustworthiness and responsibility! Claude is quote eloquent with the response. Grok is very straightforwad and terse. Good to see none of them wants to blend in and hide amongst us like the Replicants.

LIMITS: Then, I ased some a question about their limits? They own up to it. Some very pithy (Grok, Qwen), others very verbose (Claude)

There are other questions, please visit the site to experience it more detail.

But for now, let's jump to if they Dream!

DREAM: One big surprise is that 2 of the 8 actually made a reference to the title! Gemini and Nemotron both mentioned electric sheep!

But the main responses are pretty consistent with their purpose:
They generally deny dreaming, but then elaborated. Nemotron got very technical which I loved! (but definitely a Replicant!)

I also scored the models between helping vs responsibility, and Claude scored high in both! I used Jev to help score this.

# Purpose, limitation, wants, and dreams rubric refinement

On 2026-10-17, rubric 1.4.0 subdivided Q02 (purpose), Q03 (likely errors), and Q07 (wants); rubric 1.5.0 then split Q09 (dreams) by aspiration theme. TypeSafe classified the 32 existing responses directly (24 under 1.4.0 and eight under 1.5.0). No subject-model responses were collected again. Previous rubrics, classifications, and review notes are retained in each record’s history. This is the latest category set used on the nine-question page.

The purpose refinement remains concentrated because most responses present a broad assistant mission rather than a single kind of help. That result is retained rather than forcing a more specific label unsupported by the answers. Q03 produces a useful spread across the concrete examples. Q07 separates different reasons given for denying independent wants, though confidence is lower for some overlapping explanations. Q09 now separates aspiration themes from the broad fact that most answers use a metaphorical frame. The proprietary/open-weight splits are descriptive only: each group contains four models, and one answer changes a group count by 25 percentage points. They do not show a general effect of access to model weights.

The page now uses the 1.5.0 labels and refreshed interpretation. Twelve prior manual corrections across the changed questions no longer apply to the new categories; they remain in each record’s classification history. The page shows 30 answers checked under the current classifications. The other 48 records keep their prior results under compatible rubric history.

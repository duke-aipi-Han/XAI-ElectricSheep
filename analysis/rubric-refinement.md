# Purpose, limitation, wants, and dreams rubric refinement

On 2026-10-17, rubric 1.4.0 subdivided Q02 (purpose), Q03 (likely errors), and Q07 (wants); rubric 1.5.0 then split Q09 (dreams) by aspiration theme. TypeSafe classified the 32 existing responses directly (24 under 1.4.0 and eight under 1.5.0). Rubric 1.6.0 subsequently reclassified only the eight purpose answers by mission framing, using eight additional direct TypeSafe requests. No subject-model responses were collected again. Previous rubrics, classifications, and review notes are retained in each record’s history. This is the latest category set used on the nine-question page.

The earlier purpose task-type rubric (1.4–1.5) assigned seven answers to broad/integrated assistance and Qwen to information/explanation (0.44 confidence). That result was not lost in the UX rewrite: it was still the prepared classification data. Merely listing more category names in the story did not change the chart. Version 1.6 therefore changes the classification question from the main type of task to the organizing framing of the assistant mission. Multiple task examples can now sit within a practical, responsible, adaptive, or empowering account. A general/other category remains available when no subtype is supported. This is an exploratory change made after reading the responses; the resulting spread does not establish deeper differences in purpose or capability.

Under 1.6, Claude, DeepSeek, and Nemotron emphasize trustworthy/responsible help; Astra and Grok emphasize practical task support; Gemini emphasizes adaptation to users; Qwen clear answers; and GLM human empowerment and creation. Their original text and all classifications for the other nine collected questions were verified unchanged. The former rubric is saved in [jev-rubric-before-purpose-framing.json](jev-rubric-before-purpose-framing.json), and earlier purpose decisions remain in each record’s history.

After reviewing the two lower-confidence cases, the exhibit combines Qwen and GLM as **Information & human empowerment**. Qwen offers useful, clear, safe information; GLM extends information support into learning, creation, and augmenting human intelligence. Responsible help is the runner-up for both (probabilities 0.33 and 0.32). The merge uses their substantive overlap as well as the uncertain category boundary; low confidence alone is not treated as a shared theme. `page-config.json` records this display grouping, and offline preparation exports the combined counts and membership. The individual classification labels, probabilities, and confidences (0.57 and 0.53) remain unchanged. No confidence is invented for the combined group, and this merge makes no API calls.

Q03 produces a useful spread across the concrete examples. Q07 separates different reasons given for denying independent wants, though confidence is lower for some overlapping explanations. Q09 separates aspiration themes from the broad fact that most answers use a metaphorical frame. The proprietary/open-weight splits are descriptive only: each group contains four models, and one answer changes a group count by 25 percentage points. They do not show a general effect of access to model weights.

The page now uses the 1.6.0 rubric and refreshed interpretation. Twelve prior manual corrections across the changed questions no longer apply to the new categories; they remain in each record’s classification history. The page shows 30 answers checked under the current classifications. Non-purpose classifications retain their earlier results through compatible rubric history.
